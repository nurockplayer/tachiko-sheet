This operating mode is **Stewarded Continuous Delivery (SCD)**. Follow
[`docs/DELIVERY.md`](docs/DELIVERY.md) for Sheet-local authority; the shared name
does not replace its existing role, Ready, acceptance, review or merge rules.

Read the live **#1 body**, **#2 body**, `docs/DELIVERY.md`, and the active Issue/PR. Use `docs/UPSTREAM.md` to read relevant live core authority. A fresh independent exact-HEAD **Oracle latest Extra High** review is required at each Final Candidate. Use the qualified **ego-lite → Oracle** transport by default; qualify reviewer provenance using the supported visible evidence rule in `docs/DELIVERY.md` and live #1 (2026-09-29). #1 is Mission; #2 is the single current handoff, replaced in place. Do not mine #1 comments for current state. Read `docs/PRODUCT-PRINCIPLES.md` once at intake; `docs/MVP-EXECUTION.md` is the compact queue/gate map, not an instruction to reload every research source each loop.

There is one Mission Lead and integrator. The founder records **GPT-6 Sol** as the active Mission Lead in #2 after the #77 transition is merged. Sol is the highest engineering decision authority inside already-settled product, semantic, storage and acceptance boundaries. **GPT-6 Luna** carries one bounded implementation/unit-test package at a time. Claim/transfer ownership in #2 and confirm prior writers stopped before changing leaders. Preserve one writer per worktree/branch and serial production implementation.

Sol records material engineering choices, repair directions and substantive review-finding dispositions in the owning Issue/PR. A useful decision entry states the problem, chosen approach, key rationale/tradeoff, important rejected alternative when relevant, and rollback path when material. Reporting is traceability, not an approval gate; Sol may proceed on decisions already inside its authority. Workers return any new product/architecture/semantic/storage/security/data-integrity/acceptance choice to Sol instead of improvising.

Follow live [#1](https://github.com/nurockplayer/tachiko-sheet/issues/1)'s 2026-09-28 founder directive: before freezing a decision or assigning implementation on a material difficulty, ambiguity or decision question, Sol must consult **GPT-6 Astra**. The architecture triggers in #1 are a minimum, not the only reasons to consult. **Astra's disposition controls the consulted point; Sol must not override it unilaterally.** Sol remains the sole Mission Lead/integrator responsible for execution. Record the question, evidence, Astra conclusion, resulting disposition, important rejected alternatives and rollback/reversal path on the owning Issue/PR. Astra remains read-only by default, without competing writer or merge ownership. Escalate further only when Astra explicitly requests additional analysis or the question exceeds #1/founder delegation; older automatic Pro triggers are superseded. If that genuinely required escalation is unavailable, record HOLD and the exact unresolved question. Routine mechanical repair and clear reversible local choices do not require consultation unless a live #1 trigger applies. Product scope, Accepted semantic/storage/authorization authority, Steward acceptance, explicit HOLDs and founder/external gates remain with their existing owners.

At a stable Final Candidate, use one eligible fresh independent exact-HEAD **Oracle latest Extra High** review through **ego-lite** by default. Oracle is read-only and must be independent of the candidate's design, implementation and acceptance/evidence preparation. Run deterministic/hosted gates first. Capture the requested **Latest / Extra High** configuration before submission and correlate the complete response to the same review conversation/turn and exact frozen candidate. Require complete required review coverage and literal `No blocking findings.` Inspect effective backend model/effort metadata when the supported product exposes it; any contradiction blocks credit. Absence of hidden or product-unexposed effective metadata alone is not a failure or merge blocker. Missing required visible provenance, incomplete coverage, an unrecoverable response or transport/session failure without a complete recoverable verdict remains REVIEW_INCOMPLETE, not PASS. Do not probe credentials or guess private/auth endpoints. Required merge verdict is `No blocking findings.` A material commit after review invalidates the receipt. GPT-6 Pro is separately authorized judgment capacity, never a silent Oracle fallback. After all exact-head gates and the Oracle receipt pass, Sol/Conductor performs the protected merge.

At meaningful stages record durable GitHub checkpoints on the owning Issue/PR: INTAKE, PLAN/DECISION, IMPLEMENTED, VALIDATED, `FINAL_CANDIDATE <sha>`, ORACLE REVIEW, and MERGED/BLOCKED/HANDOFF. Keep #2 as the concise recoverable handoff. Do not spam per-command comments.

use the repository-local `$tachiko-sheet-scd-delivery` Skill. It owns the

owning Issue/PR, and relevant upstream authority remain superior.

Continue the qualified delivery loop across child Issues and PRs. There is no unconditional preflight-stop or ask-the-founder-between-tickets rule. Conditional readiness and Sheet-local merge authority are defined in `docs/DELIVERY.md`; upstream authority is separate. Do not author your own independent approval, weaken acceptance, bypass protections or force-push.

Use pnpm for JavaScript dependencies, explicit packageManager and a real lockfile. Do not add install lifecycle scripts, incidental toolchain migrations, secrets, remote fonts, telemetry or provider calls. Follow the installed frontend taste skill when available, adapted to dense spreadsheet ergonomics, not a landing page.

Never implement canonical state, formula/validation, semantic revision, storage codec or approval policy in frontend state. Never confuse semantic publication with durable Save or external effects. Report only checks actually run. Keep known data-integrity/security and substantive review findings blocking, including still-valid debt from merged code.
